# Kowloon and western New Territories open spaces

The 28 October western fill extends the eastern Kowloon and [Wong Tai Sin map](WONG_TAI_SIN_EXPANSION.md) with nine reviewed sites. The integrated layout contains **25 sites, 120 source sports outlines across 135 sheet fragments, 120 rendered sport surfaces, 88 intact court/field fittings, 3,068 authored fixtures and 81,836 paving triangles**. Its 116 path/access samples cover all retained paving components. The previous 16-site snapshot had 71 outlines across 81 fragments, 50 fittings, 1,505 fixtures and 44,518 paving triangles; those records remain exact in the integrated output.

The 25 sites are the original nine eastern Kowloon parks and waterfront spaces; Morse Park, Junction Road Park, Choi Hung Road Playground, Lion Rock Park, Diamond Hill Water Feature Park, Ma Chai Hang Recreation Ground and Fung Tak Park; and Hoi Fan Road Park, Central Kwai Chung Park, Shek Kip Mei Park, Lai Chi Kok Park, Nam Cheong Park, Shing Mun Valley Park, Kwai Chung Park, Tsuen Wan Park and Tsuen Wan Riviera Park. Road, building and scheduled-service geometry remains the Task 1 result.

## Integration

The game loads the integrated recreation data and separate Blender asset after Mong Kok recreation and before roadside decoration. Both regions' sport triangles share the existing terrain recreation mask. Existing Mong Kok data, fittings and play-pad behavior remain unchanged. All 25 park interiors and entrance corridors are reserved from later roadside decoration. Paving enters the shared terrain mask before street mesh completion, avoiding stacked ground meshes. The recreation mask request uses the existing density parameter to cap both axes at 8,192 pixels; the runtime check asserts RGB8 and a base buffer no larger than 192 MiB. Since the [30 October startup fix](STARTUP_PERFORMANCE.md), every terrain mask also has a 192 MiB base-pixel budget in addition to its axis limit.

The running track keeps its sourced annular surface, while its enclosed athletics infield is reserved from authored paths and furniture. This also prevents garden generation from treating a sports polygon's inner ring as an empty planting bed.

The offline dataset and exported GLB keep runtime fully offline. Existing street trees, banyans and benches supply the planting and seating; no new image generation is required.

`Tools/import_east_open_spaces.py` reads the verified whole-sheet cache and manifest to regenerate the source and layout files. `Tools/check_east_open_spaces.py CACHE_DIR MANIFEST_JSON [PRESERVATION_BASELINE_DIR]` checks geometry, official-index and archive hashes, exact Site/sports fragment multiplicity, deterministic regeneration and rejected refreshes. It tests a missing western sport fragment and removal of one of Tsuen Wan Park's two patches on `6-SE-25B`, as well as byte-preservation after invalid refreshes. Run `build('east-kowloon')` from `Tools/model_open_spaces.py` through Blender MCP to export the integrated GLB and editable blend in an isolated scene. The default `build()` still targets Mong Kok.

The strict fragment inventory expects all 54 Site and 135 sports patches, not only distinct IDs. A failed extraction or geometry review preserves both saved data files byte-for-byte. The complete previous 16-site source geometry, path/access/sample arrays, 1,505 fixtures, 71 surfaces and 50 fittings are checked against the Task 2 baseline. Every retained paving component keeps an interior connectivity sample with more than 1.1 m of edge clearance. For western sites, the generator also verifies that each retained path component reaches the public pavement network; where a component does not already connect, it tries a new entrance corridor using the existing 250 m limit and unchanged land/building/road/sports clearance rules. No western component was rejected in the final dataset. The game does not claim to show current construction access or venue opening status.
