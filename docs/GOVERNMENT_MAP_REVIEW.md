# Government map correctness review — 8 October 2026

Implementation follow-up: [government map corrections](GOVERNMENT_MAP_FIXES.md) records the restored road levels, island coast and IFC massing, plus the retained projection limitation. The findings below describe the pre-fix snapshot.

The map has a sound government-derived horizontal foundation, but its rendered geography is not yet a faithful reconstruction. The most consequential discrepancies are the older island land boundary and lost road grade separation. This was a read-only review of the current working tree; game code, map data and assets were not changed.

![Government geometry compared with the game](../evidence/government-map-review-2026-10-08/government-map-review.png)

1. **High priority: restore road grade separation.** Six interior crossings between source roads at different levels are rendered at the same height. Four are Hoi Fai Road over Lin Cheung Road: elevated sections `TD_107043` / `TD_107048` cross ground sections `TD_107061` / `TD_107062`. Two more occur where elevated link `ID_024647`, used by the Wan Chai return/scenic routes, crosses Gloucester Road sections `ID_015817` / `ID_015818`. These are crossings of the actual runtime polylines, more than one metre from their endpoints; all six also intersect in the independently retrieved source geometry. `Tools/import_tunnel.py:158` assigns every non-tunnel point `y = 0`, then combines these roads into the ground asphalt surface. This creates apparent at-grade junctions and allows movements that should pass above/below another road. Preserve the source level topology and model the selected elevated approaches with continuous ramps and separate collision. The government `ELEVATION` field is a relative level, **not a height in metres**; bridge heights would still require another source or an explicitly authored clearance.

2. **High priority: replace the island's rectangular shoreline before further waterfront detailing.** `ISLAND_BOUNDS` starts at Z = 1100 (`Tools/import_tunnel.py:35,206`), and `Tools/import_buildings.py:73,121` clips every island footprint to that rectangle. It cuts through Two IFC: **88.51% of its tower footprint** and **90.91% of its podium footprint** lie outside the game land. The tower retains only about **339 of 2,953 square world metres**. Current LandsD records independently confirm both source footprints: building CSUIDs `3445416270T20050430` and `3428516348P20050905`. The overlay shows the government shoreline and Central piers well north of this straight game boundary. Extend the land to the sourced harbour edge and regenerate complete landmark footprints, nearby roads and waterfront collision together. This is a documented earlier approximation, but it now visibly undermines the expanded Central map.

3. **Lower priority: the local coordinate formula is not exactly metre scale.** `Tools/import_map.py:25–27` applies 111,320 metres per degree to latitude. Comparing the same WGS84 positions in the official HK1980 Grid coordinate system (EPSG:2326), the game's 4,800 m northward extent measures **4,774.76 m**: a **0.529% stretch**, or **25.24 m**. The 2,700 m westward test differs by only 1.29 m. This is an approximation error, not the previously corrected HK1980/WGS84 datum shift. A future accuracy pass should use one projected grid for roads, buildings, shoreline and authored placements together; changing only new imports would introduce seams. Until then, describe the scale as approximate.

These millimetre/centimetre comparisons measure fidelity to the supplied digital geometry, **not survey accuracy on the ground**. Runtime four-metre road sampling adds a median 3.5 cm, 95th-percentile 61 cm and maximum 1.37 m deviation from stored polylines, mainly around bends. Road widths, lane placement, junction surfaces, footpaths, flat terrain, building facade details, stops, traffic lights and tunnel depth remain authored. Their real-world correctness was not established by this review.
